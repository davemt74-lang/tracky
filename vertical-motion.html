HOW TO PLAY

Choose a point goal and press Start game. Tracky highlights one section and shows a rep target from 4–10. Complete an up → down cycle inside that section to subtract one. Clear the target to score a point and receive a new section. First to your selected point goal ends the game.

The 1″ × 5″ lane uses CSS physical units. Exact physical size depends on display scaling and monitor calibration.
